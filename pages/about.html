About FCI

Faculty of Computing and Informatics, CUSTECH Osara

Our Mission

The Faculty of Computing and Informatics is committed to providing quality education in computing, information technology, and related disciplines. We nurture innovative, ethical, and technically skilled graduates equipped to tackle the challenges of a digital society.

We foster a culture of continuous learning, research, and collaboration between academia and industry.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computing education and research in Nigeria and across Africa — producing graduates who drive technological innovation, contribute to national development, and compete on a global stage.

We envision a faculty recognised for academic rigour, cutting-edge research, and strong industry partnerships.

About CUSTECH

Confluence University of Science and Technology Osara (CUSTECH) is a state university in Osara, Kogi State, Nigeria. The university was established to provide quality science and technology education, contributing to human capital development in Nigeria.

CUSTECH offers programmes across faculties including computing, engineering, sciences, and management.
