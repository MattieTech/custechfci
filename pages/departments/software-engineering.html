About the Department

The Department of Software Engineering trains students to design, build, test, and maintain high-quality software systems. The program combines engineering principles with hands-on coding, preparing graduates for the modern software industry.

Students use industry-standard tools and methodologies including Agile, DevOps, and modern frameworks, guided by faculty with academic and real-world experience.

Career Opportunities

Graduates are in high demand across many sectors:

Software ArchitectFull-Stack DeveloperDevOps EngineerQA EngineerTech LeadProject ManagerMobile DeveloperCloud Engineer

What Students Learn

Software DesignDesign patterns, UML, system architecture
Agile / ScrumSprint planning, kanban, iterative dev
DevOps & CI/CDDocker, Git, Jenkins, deployment
Web & Mobile DevReact, Node.js, Android, iOS
Software TestingUnit testing, integration, automation
Project ManagementPlanning, risk, team leadership
