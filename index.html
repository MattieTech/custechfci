Student Resources

Student Guide

Everything you need to navigate academic life at FCI successfully.

Faculty Rules
  • Minimum 75% attendance is compulsory
  • No mobile phones during lectures
  • Lab protective gear required in all sessions
  • Plagiarism leads to automatic course failure
  • Always carry your student ID on campus
  • Respect for staff and fellow students is mandatory
Exam Tips
  • Start reviewing at least two weeks before exams
  • Form study groups with classmates
  • Practice with past question papers
  • Get enough sleep the night before
  • Read all exam instructions carefully first
  • Manage time — do not get stuck on one question
How to Check Results
  • Visit the CUSTECH student portal
  • Log in with your matric number and password
  • Go to "Academic Records"
  • Select your semester and session
  • Download your result slip
  • Report any discrepancy to the exam officer
Course Registration
  • Clear all outstanding fees before registration
  • See your level adviser for course approval
  • Register before the published deadline
  • Do not exceed 24 credit units maximum
  • Confirm registration with your department
